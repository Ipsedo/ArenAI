# Hit/shell peak does not hold: the critic leads, the actor follows (analysis of 500/501/502)

Runs: 500, 501, 502 (context: run 502 at 688 k, canon_x brought back, competence not returned)

Analysis requested by Samuel 2026-11-11 (hyperparameters + reward). Finding: the hit/shell peak (22 % at D=1000) is undone because the critic's `ev` drops (0.5 → 0.2-0.3) on reaching D=1000 in all 3 runs and Δev precedes Δhit by 10-20 k (corr +0.4 at lag 1-2, ~0 at lag 0); the actor advances at constant speed (76 Adam steps/rollout, kl 0.02, clip 0.25, normalised advantages) on critic noise; σ frozen at the ceiling by a hard clamp (zero gradient) ⟹ drift goes into saturated μ (tanh attractor); the turret (credit via critic) lets go before the trigger (direct credit < λ horizon 1.8 s).

**Effective reward (code read):** hit +3 × damage fraction (0.2/hit ⟹ +0.6), kill +1, received −0.2/hit, death −1, aim shaping 0.3·(γΦ(s′)−Φ(s)) with Φ = Cauchy(2.5 m + 3°·d) of the minimal approach of a predicted shell (gravity + ray_test, 20 s flight); no fire cost; reserve 10, +2/hit, max 20, empty reserve ⟹ respawn full life (truncated, no penalty). Symmetric self-play: r_rcv = −r_hit/3 exactly. Φ̄ ≈ 0.1 (deduced from r_aim = −0.15e-3 = 0.3·(γ−1)·Φ̄); variance of ΔΦ not measured (CSV = mean only). Density at D=1000: one hit / 300 decisions / tank, 3-8 kills per 32-tank episode.

**Hyperparameters (computed effects):** rollout 300 × 32 = 9600 correlated rows, chunk 30, batch 512 ⟹ 19 minibatches × 4 epochs = 76 Adam steps actor and critic per 30 s rollout; lr 1e-4 / 3e-4 constant; kl 0.015-0.025 per rollout (target 0.05 never bites, skip = isolated minibatches > 0.075); clip fraction 0.2-0.29; advantages normalised per rollout (noise rescaled to 1 when the signal vanishes); GAE λ 0.95 at 10 Hz ⟹ direct credit horizon 1.8 s (shot → impact 0.5-1 s inside, aim seconds earlier = via the critic); γ 0.995 ⟹ 20 s; σ: Linear → torch::clamp(ln σ) → exp, **hard clamp = zero gradient at the ceiling**, 80-98 % of states at σ = 0.700 exactly ⟹ σ frozen, α (floor 0.001, negligible against advantages of scale 1) only regulates μ; CSV Hc = mean over dims (masks saturation of one dim).

**Measurements:**
- ev oscillates 0.3-0.55 all run; on reaching D=1000 it drops in the 3 runs (500: 0.45 → 0.32; 501: 0.54 → 0.29; 502: 0.52 → 0.18, deepest trough of the run); v (loss) also falls (0.07 → 0.013) since return variance drops (r_hit ÷2).
- corr(Δev_t, Δhit_t+k) (k = 0, 1, 2 windows of 10 k): 502 +0.03 / +0.39 / +0.38; 501 −0.07 / +0.38 / +0.45; 500 +0.23 / +0.08 / +0.31 ⟹ **critic drop precedes hit drop by 10-20 k**. corr(ev, hit) level +0.4-0.5; corr(ev, r_hit) inconsistent (−0.09 / −0.33 / +0.39) ⟹ ev is not only event density.
- Intermediate D jumps do not systematically break ev (only 500 D→700 and D→1000) ⟹ not the curriculum itself: the critic oscillates and the actor follows.
- Probes 502: at each drift, |μ| of one dim heads to saturation (canon_x 0.40 → 0.77 → 1.17), the turret lets go (aligned shots 5.8 → 1.3 %) while the trigger ratio holds (16.9 → 10.2 → 6.4); armed 83 → 58 → 43 % of decisions (dump returns when aim leaves).

**Mechanism retained:** when ev drops, normalised advantages are critic noise at scale 1; the actor still advances at constant speed (76 steps, kl 0.02/rollout, no decay) ⟹ random walk (already seen in 418/419). It goes toward saturated μ because σ is frozen and nothing brings μ back (target 0 acts only after crossing, 50-75 k later). Aim, credited via the critic, leaves first; the trigger, credited directly, keeps its selectivity. The peak is reached when the critic is at its best (ev 0.5), just before D=1000 changes the distribution and digs ev.

**Ruled out:** KL regime (kl ≪ target), σ (frozen, invariant), event rarity alone, seed noise for the peak/ev-trough coincidence (present in the 3 runs at the same place).

**Candidate levers (to discuss, nothing applied):** (1) actor speed tied to signal (decaying lr/epochs, or no per-rollout advantage normalisation but a sliding scale); (2) soft σ ceiling (sigmoid/tanh parametrisation of log σ) so σ carries entropy again (⟹ SoftClamp, see comparisons/2026-11-11_softclamp-sigma-soft-bound.md); (3) critic: stability before the actor (lr, epochs, capacity, or actor freeze if ev < threshold); (4) measure sd(ΔΦ) in CSV-only to quantify shaping noise; (5) free respawn at empty reserve = incentive to dump under fire.
