## Reading

Established:
- **Aim depends on the separate credit.** turret<5° rises in 430 (29 → 34 → 40 % side 550) and falls in 524 (16 → 12 → 10 %); ×4 gap at s16, monotone over 3 saves and 2 sides. The share of aligned shots follows (67 % versus 30 %).
- **The flattened CNN is not the cause of 524's failure**, and it helps at the start: at s5, 430 already has turret<5° 29 % (522 at same index: 13 %) and hit/shell 20 % (13.5 %). At s16, 430 ≡ 522 in precision (47/44 % versus 46/44 %) with a higher hit throughput (0.093/0.060 versus 0.075/0.035).
- **Skip does not separate the runs**: 430 cuts 17-24 % of the non-cannon minibatches from 180 k (7-10 % cannon side) and 524 20-33 %. New versus 522 (skip 0), common to both flattened-CNN runs.
- **524's trigger is sharper**: ratio 25-67 and p_al 0.3-0.4 versus 6-18 and 0.06-0.15 in 430. The on-axis dip of 520/522 is present in 430 (p_al 0.27 → 0.14 → 0.06), absent from 524.
- **α_fire** stays at the floor in 430; it lifts only in 524, where the gain per shot is low.

Not settled:
- 524's sharp trigger: effect of the single advantage on the trigger, or mere consequence of rare opportunities (few aligned and near states, hence all fired);
- single advantage and single KL stop remain confounded in 524; the joint threshold does not explain aim (skip comparable), but it is not isolated;
- 430 has only 17 k steps at D 1000: its cruise holding (axis recovery like 520/522) remains to be seen.
