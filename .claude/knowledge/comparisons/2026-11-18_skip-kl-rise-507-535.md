## Established

- The actor-update maths are identical between 508's code (25a75a34) and today's (trainer diff: refactor only).
- 508 → 509, only change = factorised credit: kl ×3 from the first rollout, skip appeared.
- 510-522: the per-group stop masked the regime (skip column = "rest" group = 0, the cannon skipped 10-30 %).
- The removal (523) did not bring back 508's regime: at equal target 0.05, skip 0.24 at 80 k versus 0; starting kl 0.008 versus 0.005; and above all kl grows ×6-7 with training where 508 stayed flat (×2).
- Hyperparameters changed since the removal: kl target 0.05 → 0.10 (delays skip, 0.24 → 0.09 at 80 k); shells_per_hit 5 and kills curriculum (528, no effect); canon entropy target −1 and σ canon cap 0.25 (529, no effect: 528 ≡ 529); critic (530-532, no effect); coloured noise (kl ×3 at start, skip ×6 at 80 k); lr 5e-5 (shifts by ~40 k).

## Not settled

What separates 508 from 523 apart from the separate credit: CNN (4 blocks + spatial softmax, 384 features → 7 flattened blocks, 256 features, output 1×2), gamma 0.995 → 0.998, discrete_target_proba 0.05 → 0.01, a·|a| curve, shell economy, empty-reserve penalty. Gamma (514 vs 515) and discrete proba (519 vs 520) did not move kl under factorised credit; removing Φ did not either (524 vs 525). The CNN remains the main suspect, without direct proof: no run isolates this change.

The share of each action in the KL is measured nowhere.

Reading: μ = W·h. The head starts at ~0 (decoupled from the trunk); as ‖W‖ grows, each Adam step on the trunk moves μ more, and the per-step KL follows (‖W_a‖ / σ_a)². The per-action split of 534 s3 fits: (0.113/0.3)² : (0.278/0.3)² : (0.176/0.19)² : (0.214/0.19)² ≈ 1 : 6 : 6 : 9 for 1 : 5 : 10 : 10 measured (wheel σ assumed ~0.3). Since 523 the head grows twice as fast as in 507/508; 526 (batch 2400) is the only recent run that stayed at 508's level. Nothing bounds this norm.
