## Reading

- Far shooting is common to the three runs: half of the shells leave with the best-aligned target beyond
  200 m, hit/shell 0.04-0.10. It is not what separates 556.
- 556 is better aligned in yaw at close range (1390 shells with |yaw| < 3° vs 971 / 677) but its gun is too
  low when it fires: pitch error > +2° on 57 % of them, hit/shell 0.36 there. Replacing 556's close-range
  hit/shell (0.42) by 554's (0.58) on its 36 % of close shells gives 0.185 + 0.36 × 0.16 ≈ 0.24 ≈ 554's 0.247.
- 556 fires more at every range (+25 % vs 554, ×2.2 vs 555) and opens the trigger when aligned at any
  distance (p_fire aligned > 200 m 0.11 vs 0.03). With +2 shells per hit and a lower hit/shell, the reserve
  empties earlier.

## Established / not established

Established on these saves: the specific deficit of 556 is gun elevation at fire time on close targets plus
shot volume; not the range of its shots. Not established: attribution to the mask encoder (one seed,
confounded changes, unequal index); what makes the gun rest low.
