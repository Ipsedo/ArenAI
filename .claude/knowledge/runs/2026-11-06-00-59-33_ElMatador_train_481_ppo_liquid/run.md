- 2026-11-06 why (mechanism, quantified on shots of 480 save_8 and 478 save_3): per-shell Cauchy is very asymmetric — mean 0.076, median 0.025, **77 % of shots under the mean**, 14 % above 2× the mean. Centred by the mean of its own shots, the median shot is negative. While paid at impact, diluted by GAE over ~15 decisions and drowned in the hit lottery, it exerted no net force on the level of p_fire (drift 477/478/480). Paid exactly at the decision it becomes a **ratchet**: p_fire falls in states under the bar (the majority), the remaining shots are better, the bar (EMA on executed shots) rises, new states pass under the bar… down to zero. A baseline tracking its own shots always puts half the shot mass under zero; with exact attribution the optimum is not to fire. Not a uniform cost (465/471/479) but same result. The « aim at fire » lever (exact attribution) is good and mechanically confirmed (policy reacts in 50 k); it is the **centring reference** to change, not a return to impact. Options submitted 2026-11-06: (B1) bar = EMA of predicted aim over **all** armed decisions, fired or not (« fire when better than usual »): independent of fire decisions, no ratchet, optimum = fire in states above the mean opportunity; cost = predict_aim every decision (measured negligible for Φ on 2026-11-03); (B2) constant frozen bar (e.g. mean of first windows = untrained turret): no ratchet but premium (dump) or tax risk depending on the value and curriculum.
