## Reading

- **Oscillating regime, like 513**: save_20 and save_23 are good (ratio 11-15 at 1000, reserve 16-19 s, fire 0.67/s). save_21 relapses, and save_22 only half recovers: trigger rises (p_arm ×2), fire accelerates (1.05/s), reserve empties at 6-9 s, disarmed share at 550 doubles (27 %) and hit/shell loses 4-6 points. Amplitude is that of 513 save_17 → save_18. Save_21's σ canon trough (0.20/0.16 at 550) coincides with the relapse.
- **What does not move**: turret stays at 26-28 % (550) and 19-23 % (1000), share of aligned shots at 40-53 %, and aligned-shot precision (h<5°) at 28-34 %. Aim is stable: only the trigger level oscillates.
- **Mean of the 4 saves**: at 550, ratio 4.8, hit/shell 19 %, h<5° 31 %, turret 27 %. At 1000, ratio 10, hit/shell 17 %, h<5° 31 %, turret 20 %. At 510 save_28's level (21/19.5 %, 34 %), slightly under in precision, and above 513 save_18 in hit/shell.

## Established / noise

- Established: save_23 is not an isolated record. 514 holds a selective trigger and 510-level aim over 80 k at D=1000, with transient trigger relapses (save_21-22).
